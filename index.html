HOW TO PLAY

Build an automaton.

Place states anywhere. Connect them with arrows labeled from the challenge’s alphabet. Your machine reads a string one symbol at a time.

  • Navigate: drag empty space in Move mode, hold Space and drag, or use the middle mouse button. Scroll to pan; Ctrl/Cmd + scroll or trackpad pinch to zoom. Use Fit to bring all states into view.
  • Move: drag a state, or click it to change its settings.
  • Place state: click an empty spot on the canvas.
  • Connect: click a source, then a destination. Click the same state twice for a loop.
  • Cancel: press Escape, right-click, or switch tools.
  • Accept: double-click a state, or use its settings. Orange states accept when the input ends there.
  • Edit arrows: click an arrow’s label to change or delete it.

DFA: one outgoing transition per symbol at most; a missing transition rejects. NFA: multiple outgoing transitions with the same symbol are allowed. All matching branches run together. A branch with no matching transition dies; others continue. An ε-transition consumes no input, and ε-cycles are handled without looping forever. After the entire input, at least one active accepting state is required. Reaching an accepting state early is not enough. Paths at the same state merge into one highlighted state. Check compares the complete language, not just sample strings.
